Security

Last updated 27 November 2026

What we actually do to protect a mailbox, and what we do not claim. This page describes the product as it is today, not a promise about the future.

The password

  • Your password is held by Firebase Authentication, not in our own database.
  • We never display it, and no screen of ours can read it.
  • The app tells your keyboard not to capitalise or autocorrect it, so what you type is what is checked.

The session

  • Signing in gives a token that expires in about an hour, and the app renews it in the background.
  • Sign out of all devices is one button, and it clears the tokens on every phone and browser.
  • While you are signed out, the Android app blocks screenshots and hides its recents thumbnail, so a typed password cannot be captured from the task switcher.

Your mail

  • Mail is read from the server, one page at a time, with your token.
  • The service worker never caches a message body, so mail is not left on a shared computer.
  • Deleting a message for good is done by the server, which also returns the space, so a browser rule cannot half do it.

Our side

  • Keys and service credentials live only in the server environment, never in the code and never in the app.
  • The app holds only the public Firebase key, which is meant to be public.
  • Sign in, signup, password reset and sending are rate limited per person, so a script cannot guess a password or flood a mailbox.
  • The legal pages carry a policy that blocks plugins, framing and base changes, and they send no referrer, so a sign in link cannot leak its token to another site.

What we do not claim

No service can say it is impossible to break into. We do not claim that, and we will not. What we claim is that the protections above are in place and are checked, and that when we find a fault we fix it and say so.

If something goes wrong

Write to support@eyemail.in. If we ever suffer a breach that affects your data, we will tell you what happened and what to do, without waiting to be asked.
