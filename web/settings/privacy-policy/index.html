Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 November 2026

This page says what we hold about you, why, and who else touches it. It is written for India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and it applies to the web mailbox and the Android app alike.

What we hold

DataWhy
Your name, as you typed itTo show on mail you send
Your address, yourname@eyemail.inIt is your mailbox and your account id
A recovery address you give usPassword reset and verification codes
Your password, held by our sign in providerTo sign you in. We never read or show it
Your mail: sender, recipients, subject, body, folders, labelsTo run the mailbox
A device token for notificationsTo tell your phone a mail arrived, if you turned alerts on
How much space you use, and daily send countsTo apply the free plan limits and stop abuse

What we do not hold

We do not ask for, and do not store, your date of birth, gender, photograph, home or work address, or any identity document. We do not build an advertising profile, and we do not sell data.

Who else touches it

ProviderWhat it does
Google FirebaseSign in, and the databases that hold your profile and your mail
CloudflareReceives mail addressed to eyemail.in and hands it to us
BrevoSends the mail you write
RenderRuns the service that connects the parts

Each of them processes data only to provide that function. We do not give your data to anyone else unless the law requires it, and if that happens we will tell you unless we are barred from doing so.

How long we keep it

  • Your mail: until you delete it. Trash empties itself after 30 days.
  • Your profile: until you delete your account.
  • Notification tokens: until you turn alerts off, sign out, or delete the account.
  • Counters used to stop abuse: kept briefly, then dropped.

Cookies and storage in your browser

We use your browser's own storage for your session, your theme, and your draft while you type. We do not use advertising cookies or third party trackers. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Children

Eyemail is not for anyone under 18. If we learn that a mailbox belongs to a child, we will close it.

Your rights

See, correct, export and delete your data. Data and Privacy says how, in one page.

Contact

Write to support@eyemail.in. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
