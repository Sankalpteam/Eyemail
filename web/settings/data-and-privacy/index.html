Data and Privacy

Last updated 27 November 2026

Your data is yours. This page is the one place that says how to see it, take it, correct it or remove it, and who to write to.

See it

Settings, then Account, shows your name, your address, your recovery address and the space you use. Every message you hold is in the mailbox, under its folder.

Take it with you

Ask us and we will give you a file of your mail and your profile. Write to support@eyemail.in from your Eyemail address, with the subject Export, and we will reply with the file.

Correct it

Name and signature you change yourself in Settings. Your recovery address you change in Settings with a code sent to the new address. Your address itself cannot be renamed, because it is your account id.

Delete it

Settings, then Account, then Delete my account. It removes the mailbox, its mail, the login and the notification tokens, and it cannot be undone. Single messages you delete yourself, and Trash empties after 30 days on its own.

Turn off notifications

Settings, then Mail alerts, turns them off, and the device token is removed so no further alert is sent to that phone.

How fast we answer

We answer a data request within 30 days, and usually much sooner. We may ask you to write from the mailbox itself, so that we know the request is yours and not someone else's.

If you are not satisfied

Tell us first, at support@eyemail.in, and give us a chance to put it right. If the answer does not satisfy you, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What this page does not cover

It does not repeat the full list of data we hold, which is on the Privacy Policy page, or the protections in place, which are on the Security page. The rules for using the service are in the Terms of Use.
